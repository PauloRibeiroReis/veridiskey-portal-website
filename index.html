The Problem

When organisations cannot verify trust

Every day, across every European city, infrastructure maintenance is performed by contractors whose qualifications have not been properly verified — because the system to do so is slow, fragmented, and built on paper.

8 April 2024 · Lisbon, Portugal

The Glória Elevator — A Visible Failure of Accountability

When the Elevador da Glória derailed and injured passengers, a critical question emerged: did the maintenance company hold the correct qualifications? It proved impossible to answer quickly. Documentation existed somewhere — in email attachments, PDFs, filing cabinets — but there was no single, verifiable, real-time source of truth. This is not an isolated failure. It is a systemic problem across every European city.

14 August 2018 · Genoa, Italy

Morandi Bridge Collapse — 43 Lives Lost

The private concessionaire had been aware of structural risks but failed to act. The maintenance company also owned the inspection company — a self-regulated system with no independent verification or tamper-proof record of accountability.

14 June 2017 · London, United Kingdom

Grenfell Tower Fire — 72 Lives Lost

Contractors failed to meet building regulations during a refurbishment. Certification of cladding materials was inadequate. No system existed to verify every contractor and material across the entire sub-contractor chain.

📄

Paper-Based Credentials

Qualifications stored in PDFs and email folders with no central, real-time verification layer. Easy to forge, impossible to track at scale.

⏱️

3–5 Days Per Verification

Procurement officers spend 15–20 hours weekly chasing PDFs, emailing supply chains, and cross-referencing spreadsheets to check a single expired policy.

🔗

Invisible Sub-contractor Chains

The winning bidder may be checked — but their sub-contractors are invisible. The most critical blind spot in risk management for any public authority.

🔒

No Tamper-Proof Audit Trail

When incidents happen, there is no immutable record to establish responsibility — officers must search old emails to prove they verified a PDF years ago.

💰

Procurement Leakage

Councils pay non-compliant contractors because no system locks payments to verified compliance. In a €6.5M project, even 2% leakage means €130,000 lost per contract.

🌍

€2 Trillion at Stake

European public procurement exceeds €2 trillion annually. This fragmented system affects every municipality, concessionaire, and infrastructure operator on the continent.

Cross-Sector Impact

One platform. Five dimensions of digital governance.

Veridiskey enables cities to demonstrate that their governance is evolving — using tamper-proof digital infrastructure that citizens and auditors can independently verify.

01 · Digitalisation

Procure-to-Pay Command Centre

Connecting Procurement, Finance, and Operations into a single immutable layer. Smart Invoice Gates lock invoices until 100% of compliance gates are verified by the responsible officer — eliminating overpayments, fraud, and audit-preparation time.

02 · Social Inclusion

Enforcing Social Value Pledges

Contractors win bids by promising local jobs and community investment. Veridiskey hard-codes those commitments as locked deliverables — payment milestones are cryptographically blocked until the responsible officer verifies evidence of delivery.

03 · Critical Infrastructure

Zero-Trust Supply Chain Security

Before any contractor is granted physical or digital access to critical city assets, they must clear the Veridiskey ledger. If a cybersecurity or safety certification expires, the system instantly flags the risk to the authority's dashboard.

04 · Climate Action

Green Retrofit Verification

Cities invest millions in energy retrofits but have no immutable proof that contractors use compliant materials. Veridiskey demands specific environmental evidence — sealed with cryptographic hashes that auditors and citizens can independently verify.

05 · Urban Mobility

Dynamic SLA Enforcement

Long-term maintenance contracts for EV charging, transit hubs, or LED streetlighting are notoriously difficult to monitor. Veridiskey integrates SLA ledgers — missed response windows trigger automatic penalty calculation, deducted from the next verified invoice. Absolute accountability in critical mobility infrastructure.

How It Works

Trusted Digital Infrastructure

Veridiskey uses a distributed ledger — a shared, tamper-proof record maintained across a network of European institutions. Every credential verification, contract milestone, and payment authorisation is permanently recorded. No single party — not the contractor, not the municipality, not Veridiskey — can alter the history.

This is not cryptocurrency. Veridiskey runs on a permissioned network — no mining, no tokens, no energy waste. Validated by trusted European institutions using Proof of Authority consensus.

European Public Infrastructure

Built on EBSI

The European Blockchain Services Infrastructure (EBSI) is a pan-European network created by the European Commission for trusted digital public services. Governed by EUROPEUM-EDIC — Portugal is a founding member.

Veridiskey is EBSI-native. Credentials follow the W3C Verifiable Credentials standard — interoperable across all 27+ EU Member States. A contractor verified in Lisbon is instantly verifiable in Madrid, Berlin, or Paris.

Your Data Stays Yours

Personal data never touches the shared ledger — only cryptographic proofs. GDPR compliance is an architectural feature, not an added layer. Credentials live on EU public infrastructure (EBSI) maintained by Member States. If Veridiskey ceases to exist, your credentials and audit trail remain valid and verifiable on the European network — your data sovereignty is guaranteed by design.
